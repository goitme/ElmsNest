# مكتبة الهوكات

أول ٣ ثوانٍ / أول سطر. الهوك يقرر إن كان الإعلان سيُقرأ أصلاً.

**القاعدة:** ابدأ بمشهد ملموس، لا بسؤال بلاغي.
❌ "Looking for the perfect gift?" — هذا افتتاح ميت، الجميع يستخدمه.

## مشهد ملموس

- "There are 847 photos of my daughter on my phone. Not one of them is on a shelf."
- "My son named his bear before I even took it out of the box."
- "She ran her thumb over the engraving and didn't say anything for a while."

## الحرفة (فيديو)

- "This is a block of oak. In 40 seconds it's going to have my daughter's name on it."
- "The laser goes 2mm deep. That's the difference between a gift and a sticker."

## المقارنة

- "$35 personalized gifts are usually plastic. This one isn't."
- "Printed names fade. Carved ones don't."

## المُهدي

- "My mom has everything. So I gave her something that didn't exist until last week."
- "The gift they open last is the one they keep."

## الاعتراض كهوك
