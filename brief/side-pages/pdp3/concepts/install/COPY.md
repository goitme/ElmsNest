# Concept «install» — every Hebrew sentence, per section, with the gate it lives under

Rules kept: short; claim-free (no hours, lumens, IP, «best», reviews, counts, urgency); no «+», no «;»; labels without a terminal period; digits/Latin inside `<bdi dir="ltr">`; nothing the page already says (kicker, price, suits/not-for, the scene's place line, the dusk row's two sentences, the facts, terms, contact).

## Section 2 — `elmsnest-s-pdp-weather` («בחוץ»)
