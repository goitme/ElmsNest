# Concept «pair» — the photographs

**Three files, and the same three on every product page** (A, B and C are identical here), so the
BRIEF §4 cap of ≤ 3 new photographs per page is met with nothing to swap per family. All three are
real photographs from the round-3 shortlist, cropped only by CSS `object-fit` / `object-position` or
by a CSS pan inside `overflow:hidden` — no file is edited, resampled or filtered.

Two of the three are CC0, so exactly **one** credit line is drawn on the page.

## Checks against BRIEF §4

- Licences: CC0 · CC BY 2.0 · CC0 — all commercial-safe; the one CC BY carries its credit verbatim from `images/SHORTLIST.md`, never omitted.
- No faces, no brand marks, no baked-in text, no watermark, no colour filter in any of the three.
- Long side: 1920 · 2047 · 3840 px — all at or above the 1200 px floor (2 and 3 are the re-fetched originals).
- No photograph is presented as this product: every one of the three carries a caption naming the place or the frame it is.
- Nothing here is from the store's own frames, so the round-2 scene is not repeated.
- The lookalike risks the shortlist flags (the alley's wall lantern) are cropped out, not argued away.
