Built 2026-09-07 from the raters' picks (pdp3 round, total ≥ 16 of 25: 73 files) plus the home round's unused internet pool (home2, total ≥ 14, 42 files). Every one of the 115 was looked at; 24 borderline frames were re-checked at ~800 px. The home page deployed store-owned frames only (`../../home2/images/CHOSEN.md`), so nothing below has appeared on the site. Ratings, licences and source pages: `ratings.jsonl`, `manifest.jsonl` (home2 ids: `../../home2/images/…`). Contact sheet of the shortlist: `contact-sheet.png`.

Selection rules applied (BRIEF §2, §4): real photographs only; commercial-safe licences; no faces, no baked text, no brand marks, no heavy filters; ≥ 1200 px on the long side preferred (the 1024 px Openverse files are flagged — their Flickr/LoC originals are larger and must be re-fetched before full-width use); Israeli or Mediterranean settings first; **nothing that could pass for a product the store sells** unless the role says so and the caption names the place.
