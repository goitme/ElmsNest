# Collection page — the creative director's critique (2026-09-03)

*Lens: the person who rejected this store's first attempt as "from the nineties, everything repeated, trivial
text, no creativity, no innovation, no creative visual design", and who has already approved the homepage and
the product page. Judged from the deployed renders only — the folds of all five URLs first, then each full page
sliced. Extends `CRITIQUE-lead.md`; does not re-run its checks (honesty rules, `bdi`, tap targets, no-JS
narrowing, counts, the shopper journey).*

---

## Verdict

This is not the nineties page and it is not a template with a photograph swapped in — the fold is genuinely the
best first screen in the project, the ledger is a beautiful piece of information design, and the ruler at a
chosen stop is the best conversion device anyone here has built. But it is not yet world-class, for three
reasons that are all visible in a still. **First, the one idea is switched off by default**: the ruler ships on
`הכל`, and in that state three of decor's four rows print the identical string `מ־89.90 ₪` with the ₪-per-metre
column suppressed — the instrument that justifies the whole page tells the visitor nothing until they tap it, so
every screenshot, every share and every search snapshot of this page shows the same number three times, which is
literally the charge I made in round 0. **Second, the page is mostly darkness**: after the fold, measured ink
density per 900 px screen falls to 0.9–2.9 % on nine of decor's twelve screens and stays between 1.2 % and 3.8 %
for eight consecutive screens on spot, against a homepage median of 28 % and a product-page median of 11 % — the
bands do not repeat one another's composition, they simply run out of material and leave black. **Third, the
craft slips where nobody looked**: the "pull quote" is an auto-generated arithmetic sentence set in 46 px serif
inside the one solid box on the page, the do-not list's named cream studio cut-outs ship at 511–691 px on
`/collections/all`, the pin tag truncates its product name with an ellipsis on three URLs, and the LEAD-01 fix
reached two of the five URLs. Kill the quote band and the one-lamp band-05, default the ruler to a real stop,
and this page is as good as the homepage. Leave them and the owner will open `/collections/all`, scroll past six
consecutive near-black screens, and say it again.

## The weakest screen, and what I would kill

**Weakest: `coll-decor`, band 05 — `05 · נמדדת לפי שטח`, CSS y 6257–7049** (`scratchpad/cd2/coll-decor-d-05.jpg`).
A 95 px gold numeral and a 60 px serif heading announce a whole category of measurement — *measured by area* — and
what follows is **one** product, shown as a 300 px line drawing of a grid, on an otherwise empty 1440 × 790 black
field. Ink density 0.9 %: ninety-nine percent of that screen is ground. It is a section header with no section
under it. Runner-up: `coll-all` band 04, where the two biggest images on the entire page are two **identical**
clip-art wall boxes side by side at ~600 px each (`scratchpad/shots/coll-all-1440-13.png`), directly under a
511 px grey daylight studio wall.

**What I would kill, in order:**
1. **The quote band, on all five URLs.** A generated sentence about `min` and `max` in a box is the round-0
   charge in one object. Its 780 px and its one product go into the diptych.
2. **Decor band 05 and any band that holds a single product.** A band is a composition; one card is a card.
   Merge single-product bands into their neighbour.
3. **The `הכל` default on the ruler.** Not the control — the default. Ship the page pre-answered.
4. **The two cream wall cut-outs and the birch on `/collections/all`.** The do-not list named them; the glyph
   plate exists precisely so they do not have to ship.

## What is genuinely good — do not break this

1. **The ruler at a chosen stop.** Tap `6 מ׳` and five ranges become four priced answers with a ₪-per-metre
   figure beside each — `≈13.83`, `≈19.98`, `≈12.84`, `≈22.49` — and the garland that cannot reach dims and
   prints how far it does. It is pure CSS, it works with scripts off, and no lighting shop I know of does it.
   Fix only its default state; do not touch the mechanism.
2. **The measure ledger (`טבלת המידות`).** Fourteen numbered hairline rows, tabular numerals, no motion, every
   length and every price taken from a real variant, and a foot that names the three products measured
   otherwise instead of forcing them onto the rail. It is the densest and most confident screen on the page and
   it should be moved *up*, not softened.
3. **The fold, on all five URLs and both viewports.** Place, a lit lamp pinned where it would actually stand, a
   real price, a route to buy — and, on decor, `≈17.98 ₪ למטר` at the moment of arrival. Nothing in this
   critique asks for a pixel of it to change except the missing jump line on three URLs (CREATIVE-06).
