# Collection page — front-end QA (keyboard · reduced motion · no-JS · 320 / 390×664 · SR semantics · code)

Lens: front-end QA engineer. Everything below was **executed**, not read: Playwright (chromium 1194,
`--no-sandbox`) against the five served mirrors in `brief/inventory/coll-{decor,path,wall,spot,all}`, plus a
source audit of `theme/sections/elmsnest-v2-coll-*.liquid`, `theme/snippets/elmsnest-v2-coll-*.liquid` and
`theme/templates/collection.json`. Evidence images are in `brief/side-pages/collection/qa/`.
I do not repeat what `CRITIQUE-lead.md` established (honesty rules, `<bdi>`, tap targets, the no-JS narrowing,
the counts, the shopper journey) — this extends it.

## Verdict

Five of my six suites pass cleanly and two of them pass exceptionally — reduced motion is honoured to the pixel,
and the JS-off render of `coll-decor` is **byte-identical** to the JS-on render at three scroll depths (MAD 0.000000
over 390×844×3), which is the strongest no-JS proof I have measured on this project; but the scene's pinned tag is
positioned in photo-percent while the type block grows upward from the fold, so on **any viewport shorter than
~700 px the tag prints across the eyebrow and the `h1`** — at 390×664 on `coll-decor` the collection's own name and
its eyebrow interleave into unreadable text, and at 320 the tag lands across the middle of `coll-spot`'s three-line
`h1`. That is the one thing on this page an owner would reject on sight, and it is a positioning bug, not a redesign.
Everything else I found is minor: an 8 px focus ring on the scene pin, a lamp name truncated to 31 % of itself at
11 px, two links to the same anchor inside one screen, a 12-word sentence used as the ruler's accessible name in
price mode, and an `h1 → h3` heading jump on all five URLs.

## What is genuinely good (do not break it)

1. **The no-JS contract is not a claim, it is a pixel identity.** JS-off and JS-on renders of `coll-decor` at
   390×844 differ by **zero** pixels at the fold, at the ruler and at the bands. Whatever gets changed to fix
   QA-01, re-run that diff — this is the property that makes the whole CSS-only ruler defensible.
2. **The reduced-motion branch is complete, not decorative.** Every lamp lit, `opacity:1`, and literally zero
   transitioning elements in the collection sections on all five URLs, while the same pages animate 101–264
   elements without the preference. Nothing needs to be added here; something could easily be lost.
3. **The illustration plate's accessible name.** `<svg role="img" aria-label="<the real product title> — איור,
   אין תצלום נקי">` tells a blind visitor both what the lamp is and that they are being shown a drawing, not a
   photograph. Fifteen products depend on it. Only QA-09's duplicate caption needs touching; the label itself
   is the best-written string on the page.
