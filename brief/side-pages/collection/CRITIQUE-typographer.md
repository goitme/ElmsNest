# Collection page — Hebrew typography critique

**Lens.** Hebrew typographer for premium Israeli brands. Judged on the deployed dev-theme renders
(`brief/inventory/coll-{decor,path,wall,spot,all}/http-{desktop,mobile}.png`) at 1440 and 390, and on the
served mirrors driven with Playwright — with the real Frank Ruhl Libre and Heebo faces injected from
`brief/assets/fonts/` so line-breaking in my measurements matches the deployed page byte for byte (the h1 on
`coll-spot` measures 246 px tall at `line-height:81.85px` = exactly the three lines the deployed PNG shows).
Every claim about markup is confirmed by grep on the mirrored `index.html`; every claim about a line box is a
`Range.getBoundingClientRect()` measurement, not an eyeball. I did not re-run what `CRITIQUE-lead.md`
established (honesty, `<bdi>` presence, tap targets, no-JS narrowing, counts, the shopper journey).
Evidence crops I produced are in `brief/side-pages/collection/typographer/`; the ones whose names end `-s` are
scaled for reading, the coordinates quoted are always logical CSS pixels.

## Verdict

The Hebrew *micro*-typography here is genuinely excellent and better than any Shopify Hebrew theme I have
audited — the bidi is right glyph by glyph, the geresh/maqaf/en-dash/em-dash/middot system is applied without
a single lapse across five pages, and the prices form a real tabular column down the desktop rail — but the
*macro* typography is undone by one four-word CSS rule: `.env2-coll-scene__h1b{display:block}` puts a hard
line break in front of the last word of every collection title, which is why `תאורת קיר` — nine characters
that occupy 325 px of a 640 px measure — is set on two lines at 84 px, why `ספוטים, פרוז׳קטורים ותאורה ניידת`
breaks 250 / 627 / 165 px and strands `ניידת` in glow on a third line, and why `/collections/all` has no glow
at all; add to that the one running paragraph on the page set at 76 characters per line inside a design system
that caps every other paragraph at 38ch, and product names set at 11 px with .16em tracking and
`text-transform:uppercase` on the pin tags, and the page reads as beautifully engineered type inside a
carelessly specified display layer. Four major findings, none of them a redesign; all four are CSS-only fixes
that a fixer can land in an afternoon.

## What is genuinely good — do not break these in the fix round

1. **The bidi is right glyph by glyph, and I checked it that way rather than trusting the markup.** Reading every mixed string's visual order character by character: `מ־69.90 ₪` → `₪69.90־מ`; `כמות · 1–12 יח׳` → `׳חי1–12·תומכ`; `7 מנורות בעמוד אחד. אין עמוד 2.` → `.2דומעןיא.דחאדומעבתורונמ7`; `5–22 מ׳` renders with the 5 to the left of the 22 inside its own LTR island. The area dimensions carry an explicit `<bdi dir="ltr">12×2</bdi>` — the one construction that would otherwise reverse to `2×12`, because `×` is a neutral between two numbers and resolves RTL — and `<bdi>1–12</bdi>&nbsp;יח׳` uses a non-breaking space to keep the unit with its figure. That is not luck.
2. **The punctuation system is applied without a single lapse across five pages.** Zero ASCII apostrophes after a Hebrew letter, zero ASCII quotes, zero hyphen-minus between Hebrew letters, zero hyphen ranges. Geresh U+05F3 (71 on decor), maqaf U+05BE (19–50), en dash for numeric ranges, em dash for parentheticals, middot for lists — one consistent, correct set. Israeli sites at four times this budget get this wrong.
3. **The desktop price column is a real tabular column, and no product title is set in the serif anywhere.** Every `.env2-price` in the ruler starts at exactly x=236 on decor and on path, so with FRL 500 `tabular-nums`, `white-space:nowrap` and two decimals on every price, the ₪ signs and the decimal points stack down the rail; `89.90` and `139.90` and `549.90` line up. And an audit of every leaf text node in all seven sections on all five URLs found zero product titles in Frank Ruhl Libre — the §6.11 rule holds everywhere, including the ledger, the span rungs and the `/all` entries.
