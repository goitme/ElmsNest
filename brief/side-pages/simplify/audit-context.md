## Design system of the dev theme (for reference)
Dusk→night sky gradient ground, Frank Ruhl Libre (serif display) + Heebo, gold #e9b96e, "lamps" that light on arrival, radius 0, hairlines not boxes, no grids of equal cells, "eight sections eight compositions".
