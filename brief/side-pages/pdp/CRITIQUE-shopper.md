# CRITIQUE — the shopper's walk (mobile 390×844, Israeli buyer, impatient and sceptical)

*Lens: I want string lights for my terrace. I drove the three deployed archetypes on a served mirror
with Playwright at 390×844 (DPR 2, touch, he-IL), stubbed `/cart/add.js` · `/cart.js` · `/cart/update.js`,
and tried to actually buy. Every PNG, measurement and file:line below is one I produced myself.
Screenshots live in `brief/side-pages/pdp/audit-shopper2/`.*

---

## Verdict

I found the price in under a second, understood in about five that "24 variants" means six lengths at six
prices and four colours that cost the same, and had a real `הוספה לסל` under my thumb without scrolling —
that part of this page is better than anything I have used on an Israeli lighting site. Then it broke my
trust twice. I tapped `11 מ׳` on the rail; the top of the page and the sticky bar both moved to
`109.90 ₪ · 11 מ׳`, but when I scrolled down to the ledger — the section built to make exactly this choice —
it told me my choice was `5 מ׳ / 20 נורות · 89.90 ₪`, and its button posted the 5-metre variant
(same on the path light: I picked 4 units, it added 1; same on the wall light: I picked 12W, it added 6W).
And the page never once told me this lamp might not be for me: section 02 is titled `איך בוחרים נכון`,
shows only the positive half, and nothing goes dark — the switch, the approved negative and the
"how much sun does the place get?" question are all absent on all three products, so I got to the button
without ever learning that a solar string on a shaded terrace will not light. **Would I buy? Not today.**
I would leave at the ledger, because a page that contradicts itself about what I chose is a page I do not
trust with my card — and if I had bought, I would have bought the wrong length and blamed the shop.
Fix the one-way binding and put the negative back, and I would buy the 11-metre in one sitting.

## What is genuinely good — do not break it

1. **The fold does the whole job on a phone.** Kicker, headline, product title, colour axis, a resolved
   `89.90 ₪` with the honest range line `מ־89.90 עד 179.90 ₪, לפי אורך ומספר נורות`, a full-width
   `הוספה לסל` whose bottom edge sits at 556 px (spec asked for above 700), the four consumer terms,
   the photo-check link **and** a six-stop price rail — all above 844 px, over a real night photograph of
   the product. `audit-shopper2/A-01-fold.png`. Nothing here is padding; do not add anything to it.
2. **`06 · אחרי הלחיצה` and `מה שלא כתוב`.** The terms ledger answers the four questions I would otherwise
   have hunted for in a policy page (`0₪ pickup / 29.90 courier`, `8–17 ימי עסקים`, `14 יום` with the exact
   cancellation fee, and the photo check), and the facts section ends by naming what the shop does *not*
   know — `מידות מדויקות, לומן ואחריות אינם מופיעים בנתוני המוצר — ולכן אינם מופיעים כאן`. That single line
   bought back more scepticism than any badge would have. `audit-shopper2/A-facts-crop.png`.
3. **The buy plumbing that does work.** Ledger row → stage → sticky bar is instant and correct (row tap
   moved stage, bar and hidden id together, and the POST carried the right variant); the sticky bar hides
   itself while the ledger is on screen so there are never two competing buttons; and §8.3 was obeyed —
   `a.env2-pdp-ask__start` computes `rgb(26,18,6)` on `rgb(255,211,148)`, not the 1.21:1 core bug.
