# PDP v2 — creative director's critique

**Lens.** I am the director who rejected this store's first attempt as *"from the nineties, everything repeated,
trivial text, no creativity, no innovation, no creative visual design."* My job here is to refute the claim that the
new product page is world-class. Judged from the renders only, in order: the two folds, then the full desktop and
mobile pages sliced into readable pieces, for all three archetypes — `pdp-multi` (crystal, 24 variants),
`pdp-single` (path light, 1 variant), `pdp-wall` (wall light, 8 variants, mains) — against
`brief/inventory/home/http-{desktop,mobile}.png`.

## Verdict

It is not world-class, and the reason is the same sentence the owner used about round 0. The craft is real — the ground
is night, the hairlines are clean, the ledger is honest, the fold sells — but the **page has no authored idea of its
own**: the `<h1>` on all three products (and therefore on all 27) is the compliance phrase *"מתאים כדי + <one of four
place verbs>"*, the product's own name is demoted to a 19 px paragraph, and that same phrase is then repeated five
times down one page (kicker, h1, section 02 head, three card kickers). Screen 02 — the section that carries the only
thing this store has that nobody else has, *"we will tell you when it does not suit you"* — asks «זה יתאים למרפסת
שלי?» and never answers *no*: on **all three** products it renders the suits-only branch, with a row of evenly spaced
dots where the switch should be. The ledger's "what is this length *for*" column is empty on every row of every
product. And the three pages are the same page: the eight bands land at the same rhythm, the same eight shapes, the
same y-offsets, differing only in photograph (`rhythm.png`). Measured against the homepage the PDP is a document, not
a store: 7–13 % of its pixels are lit versus the homepage's 24 %, and the third quarter of every PDP — roughly 4,000 px
of ledger-tail, facts and terms — is 2.7–2.9 % lit, i.e. grey text on black. **The weakest screen is 02 (`pdp-fit`)**:
965 px on mobile / ~2,400 px on desktop spent on ~60 words, a question it refuses to answer, a gold restatement of the
kicker, and a decorative dotted rule. **What I would kill:** the `__band` bulb rule in every archetype, the
`__only` suits-restatement, and the "מתאים כדי" h1 — and if the negative genuinely cannot be sourced, kill section 02
outright rather than ship an empty frame around the store's one differentiator.

## What is genuinely good — do not break it

1. **The fold sells, honestly, on both viewports.** Resolved price + the range line + a real `הוספה לסל` + the four
   terms in one strip, with the buy button's bottom edge at **556 px** on 390×844 (Playwright) — well clear of browser
   chrome — and the whole variant range visible on the rail before anything is selected. Prices to two decimals,
   per-metre figures to two decimals (`≈ 17.98`, `≈ 8.18`), no badge, no strikethrough, no urgency.
   (`brief/side-pages/pdp/critique-creative/multi-mfold-00.png`, `brief/side-pages/pdp/critique-creative/mD-03.png`.)
2. **The drawn string over the hero photograph, and the lit stop on the rail.** On product A the catenary painted
   across the real night photo, with the chosen stop glowing gold on its hairline, is the one moment on the page that
   only a lighting store could have made. (`brief/side-pages/pdp/critique-creative/multi-dfold-00.png`.)
3. **Honesty rendered as design.** The giant `IP65` against the `dl`, and especially the `מה שלא כתוב` row
   ("מידות מדויקות, לומן ואחריות אינם מופיעים בנתוני המוצר — ולכן אינם מופיעים כאן"), plus the terms ledger's
   "0 / 8–17 / 14 יום / 1 תמונה" and the related deck's "בלי כוכבים ובלי «נמכר ביותר» — אין לנו עדיין מה למדוד."
   That is editorial Hebrew doing work no template does. (`brief/side-pages/pdp/critique-creative/mD-04.png`, `brief/side-pages/pdp/critique-creative/mD-05.png`, `brief/side-pages/pdp/critique-creative/mD-06.png`.)

checked against §9.1: both render `rgb(26,18,6)` on `rgb(255,211,148)`, so §8.3's guard is holding.*
