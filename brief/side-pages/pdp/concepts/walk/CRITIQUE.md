# walk — הליכת לילה · self-critique

## The one idea

**The product page is a walk toward the lamp**: the same product photographed from far, from the middle of
the garden and from a hand away, as three full-bleed night moments — the words are captions on the
photographs, the buy box is a scrim card that travels with you, and between the moments there is one dark
screen carrying a single sentence: what this lamp is *not* for.

## Products B and C

- **path.html** — screen 1 with a wide scrim **ledge** (not a card): title, `169.90 ₪`, a quantity stepper
  that recomputes the total (`509.70 ₪ · 169.90 ₪ ליחידה`) because the description says
  *"הוסיפו את הכמות המתאימה לאורך הדרך"*, and the ATC. No one-value picker — the single option value is
  stated as a sentence instead. Screen 2 is the pair split by a vertical hairline
  (`לראות את הדרך` / `המקום כמעט אינו מקבל אור יום`). Screen 3 is the near photo + the fact ledger + terms.
- **wall.html** — screen 1 full-bleed façade, h1, then **one line under a rule**: title · `219.90–252.90 ₪` ·
  `לבחירת עוצמה וגוון`. Screen 2 is the stage: the lamp on a dark plate whose halo **grows when 12W is
  chosen**, beside the four W×K rows with real prices and body colour as the quiet no-price axis. Then the
  כניסה/קיר pair and the fact ledger. **No solar sentence anywhere** — the power line is `חיבור לחשמל`.

## Where it sells hardest

1. **The ledger.** Twenty-four variants become six rows you can read like a price list, with the colour
   question defused in one sentence (`הצבע לא משנה מחיר`) before it can become a second decision. The
   length bar and the lighting string make "how long is 13 מ׳" a visual answer, not arithmetic.
2. **The dark screen.** Spending a whole screen on the one thing the lamp cannot do buys the credibility
   that every other number on the page then trades on — and it is the store's position rendered as design.
3. **Buy density.** Price + action in the mobile fold, the real ATC on the close-up, a sticky bar that
   mirrors the selection, and the terms line pressed against the button.

## Where it is weakest

1. **Screen 3 is the least composed screen.** The photo/caption split is the most conventional layout on the
   page, and the seven-row fact ledger is the one place where the "almost no words" rule is broken.
2. **The dark screen risks reading as empty on desktop** — a 96 svh black field with ~10 words. It is the
   idea, but a judge scanning a full-page PNG sees a lot of nothing between two photographs.
3. **Only two usable photographs exist for product A.** The "far" moment borrows the decor collection scene,
   which is a different string of lights than the crystal balls. Honest as atmosphere, but the walk is not
   literally the same three frames of one product — with a real shoot it would be.
4. The scale cue is metres and bulb counts only; the description gives no spacing rule, so there is no
   "6 מטר ≈ N units" coverage answer for A (correctly — but it leaves a real question partly open).

## What one more day would buy

- Make the **string graphic run the full width of the close-up screen**, not just the ledger card, so the
  variant device literally lights the photograph — the strongest version of the idea, cut for time.
- Give screen 3 a **second composition**: the facts as a vertical caption laid *on* the photograph at the
  dark edge, with the photo full-bleed, so no screen has a left/right split.
- Add a **"one unit now, the set later"** device to path.html (the description supports it) and mirror the
  quantity stepper into A as *"אפשר להתחיל מהאורך הקצר"*.
- Tune the dark screen to `min-height:78svh` on desktop and let the next photograph's top edge appear at the
  bottom of it, so restraint reads as anticipation rather than a gap.
- Wire the `related` cards to the real price rule snippet and add the fourth product (a set) once a
  text-free image exists for it — today three is the honest maximum for this place.
