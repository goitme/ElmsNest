# CRITIQUE — Hebrew typographer (PDP v2, dev theme 154726400174)

## Verdict

This is a real Hebrew page, not an English page flipped: the display face is Frank Ruhl Libre and the product
titles are Heebo (never the serif), the maqaf, geresh and gershayim are the correct Unicode characters and not
ASCII stand-ins, the price snippet is one snippet with `<bdi>`, tabular figures and `nowrap` everywhere it is
used, the do-not-split-a-slash-pair rule (§6 #17) is honoured, and nothing overflows at 360 — but the type is
let down in five places that a Hebrew reader notices before a designer does: **numeric ranges written without
`<bdi>` print backwards** (`22–5 מטר` where the product is 5–22 metres, `200–20 נורות`, `10–8 שעות`), **two
desktop paragraphs are set `text-align:end`, which in RTL puts the ragged edge on the side the eye returns to**,
**thirteen information-bearing strings sit under the §3.2 desktop floor of 14 px** and the "מ־" that turns a price
into a minimum is 11.78 px on mobile, **the sticky bar clips its variant mirror mid-Latin-token** (product C
shows `מנורת קיר D…` and `6W / אור חם …`, hiding the price-bearing 3000K), and **the primary buy button is
42.8 px tall on mobile** against a 44 px floor. None of that stops a sale; all of it is a day's work. One thing
outside the page itself matters more than any of them: **every render this round was judged from was shot
without Frank Ruhl Libre and without Heebo** — see TYPOGRAPHER-01.

## Method

Measurements are mine, taken from `/home/user/ElmsNest/brief/inventory/{pdp-multi,pdp-single,pdp-wall}/index.html`
served over `http://127.0.0.1` and driven with Playwright/Chromium (`--no-sandbox`) at **1440×900** and **390×844**
(plus a 360 pass), with `/cart/add.js`, `/cart.js` and `/cart/update.js` stubbed via `page.route` and every
off-host request aborted. Bidi claims are not eyeballed: for each element I take the client rect of **every
character**, sort by x, and reconstruct the visual left-to-right glyph order, then compare it with the logical
string. Ink metrics for the leading test come from `CanvasRenderingContext2D.measureText` on the real face.
Evidence PNGs are in `brief/side-pages/pdp/critique-typographer/`.

Because the shipped mirrors have no local copy of the two brand faces (TYPOGRAPHER-01), all renders below were
re-shot with `brief/assets/fonts/{FrankRuhlLibre,Heebo}-{hebrew,latin}-*.woff2` served locally as `@font-face`
with real weights and `unicode-range`. Working renders:
`/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-ElmsNest/1c2132db-077d-58e0-b54a-35f2ebea6b2c/scratchpad/F-{multi,single,wall}-{desktop,mobile}[-fold].png`.

## What is genuinely good — do not break it

1. **The price snippet, and the slash-pair rule.** `snippets/elmsnest-v2-price.liquid` emits one shape —
   `<bdi>{n}</bdi> ₪`, number first — and `.env2-price` gives it FRL 500, `tabular-nums`, `white-space:nowrap`
   and `unicode-bidi:isolate` from one place, sized per section by `--env2-price-size` (46 / 26 / 22 / 21 / 22 px).
   Every price on all three products verified correct by character position, including the wrapping ranges
   `<bdi>129.90–159.90</bdi> ₪`. And §6 #17 is honoured: nowhere is a slash pair split across two `<bdi>`s —
   the wall title's `6W/12W` and the facts' `<bdi>6W</bdi> או <bdi>12W</bdi>` both print in the right order
   (verified per character, not assumed).
2. **The face discipline.** Product titles are Heebo 400 with `unicode-bidi:isolate` in all three places they
   appear (stage, related card, sticky bar) and never the serif — §6 #12 holds. `.env2-h em{font-style:normal}`
   stops Chromium synthesising an oblique on the glow line of every h1, which Frank Ruhl Libre has no italic for;
   that is a detail most builds miss.
3. **The Hebrew punctuation, and the small type over the photograph.** Across all three pages: maqaf ־ (U+05BE)
   ×7, geresh ׳ in `מ׳`, gershayim ״ in `ס״מ`, proper en and em dashes, and **zero** ASCII apostrophes, straight
   quotes or hyphens standing in for a maqaf. The variant strings render exactly as written —
   `5 מ׳ / 20 נורות · צהוב` and `6W / אור חם 3000K · לבן` both verified character by character. And the 13–14 px
   copy sitting on top of the hero photograph measures 8.9–17.8 : 1 against its own local background at 390,
   with no horizontal overflow at 360 on any of the three.
