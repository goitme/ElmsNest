# CRITIQUE — front-end QA
**Lens:** keyboard-only buying · `prefers-reduced-motion` · JavaScript off · 320 / 390×664 · screen-reader semantics · schema + Liquid hygiene
**Tested:** 2026-09-02, against the three JS-enabled mirrors deployed to dev theme `154726400174`
(`brief/inventory/pdp-{multi,single,wall}/index.html`).

## Verdict

The plumbing is in very good shape and the content wiring is not: a keyboard buyer reaches `הוספה לסל` in six
tabs on mobile and completes the add with the cart drawer taking focus; with JavaScript disabled every price is
visible at `opacity:1`, all six ledger rows are real `<form method="post" action="/cart/add">` with a native
`<select name="id">`, the fold button posts a variant through a `form=` binding and the page is night, not black;
under `prefers-reduced-motion:reduce` **zero** elements in the eight sections carry a non-zero transition or
animation duration and every lamp is lit; there is no horizontal page scroll at 320, 360 or 390; one `<h1>`, every
section `aria-labelledby`, every control named, every image with a real alt, and no console error beyond the two
known mirror ones — but **spine #2 renders no device at all on any of the three archetypes** (the switch, the
approved negative and the solar question are all gated on metafields that are empty live, so the section falls back
to restating the `<h1>`'s own suits phrase), the ledger's graft-A `__use` captions were never authored into the
template so spine #4 ships without "what this row is for", and two measurable defects remain: the primary
`הוספה לסל` computes **42.8 px** tall on mobile because `.env2-btn{min-height:0}` in the inline core beats the
section's own `.env2-pdp-stage__atc{min-height:52px}`, and the 11 px stage kicker measures **2.75 : 1** against the
brightest photo pixels behind it on the wall desktop fold.

## What is genuinely good — do not break it

1. **The no-JS contract is the best part of this build.** Six real `<form method="post" action="/cart/add">` rows each with a native `<select name="id">`; the stage's fold button gets its variant through a `form="env2-pdp-stage-form"` binding on a select that sits *outside* the form (so the fold stays a single visual column); the buy bar rendered and un-transformed with its own hidden `id`; `body{padding-block-end:78px}` so it never eats the footer; and `elmsnest-v2-core.liquid:286` catching a failed fetch with `form.submit()` so the native POST still fires. Nothing here is decorative — every piece of it is load-bearing.
2. **Reduced motion and the lamp guard are exactly right.** Zero transition/animation durations in the whole PDP under `reduce`, and `[data-lamp]{--lit:1}` in the media query lights every lamp without depending on the `.lit` class or on IntersectionObserver ever running — which is why the reduced-motion fold is fully lit at scroll 0. Paired with a consistent 2 px `--env2-glow` focus ring on every PDP control.
3. **Structure and the card geometry.** One `<h1>`, eight `aria-labelledby` sections, every control named, `aria-live` on all three price mirrors, zero contrast failures on flat ground, zero radius outside the pills, the terms ledger `1fr 1fr` at desktop and one column at mobile (never four across), and the related deck measuring **300 / 210 / 260 px** at aspect **0.95 / 0.71 / 1.11** — §4.8's `300/210/260` at `1/1.05`, `1/1.4`, `1/.9`, to the pixel.
