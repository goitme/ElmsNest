## 0. State of the side pages

**Collections** are every Kalles store's catalogue page — cream `#f7f0e6`, Assistant, a taupe banner, six grid toggles and a filter drawer with an English "Price" facet — under a header whose menu is invisible, with two-thirds of the featured images shouting baked-in Hebrew slogans. **The product page** already had a design pass ("PDP Design v2": ten `elms-pdp-*` sections, ~9.6k px desktop) but it is the cream/brown/`#12100e` scheme with boxes, the same ~5,000 px of template copy on every product, solar-only copy on wired lamps, a comparison table against "מנורה גנרית זולה", and a variant picker that never shows a price. **Cart, search and 404** are Kalles defaults verbatim (a beige table, an empty grey band where the query should be, letter-spaced Hebrew on the 404) plus one honest custom box-set (`elmsnest-cart-guidance`); the cart drawer was never captured. **Content pages** are one 37 KB hard-coded Liquid switch that prints five well-written cream documents with no images, no lamps and one device repeated (band → ruled list → two buttons), while `shipping-delivery` and `contact-us` fall outside the switch and render with an invisible title (and, on shipping, no gutters). **Policies** are Shopify's locked template in the old scheme at 1,410 px measure with duplicated titles and admin titles still in English. **Blog, password and customer templates** were never mirrored: the blog shows Kalles sewing-machine placeholders, the customer templates say "Login"/"Register". The gap to the new homepage is total: the homepage is the only page with `env2 base loaded: True`, `FRL font loaded: True`, a sky gradient, 17 `[data-lamp]` elements, glow prices and a legible header; every other page has `env2 base loaded: False`, `FRL font loaded: False`, `whatsapp float: False`, body scheme `scheme-1`, and ends in a hard cream→`#020306` cut into the night footer.

## 2. Shared plumbing that must exist before any side page

A page-agnostic core, rendered from `layout/theme.liquid` on every template, has to:

1. **Split `snippets/elmsnest-v2-base.liquid`** — today it is rendered by `elmsnest-v2-hero` only and its gradient targets `body.hdt-page-type-index` (l.25–39). The global part (tokens, `elmsnest-v2-fonts` FRL 500/700/900 + Heebo 300/400/500 with size-adjust fallbacks, `.env2-wrap`, pills, hairline `rgba(244,238,227,.12)`, scrim `rgba(5,8,14,.55)`+blur, `[data-lamp]` + `window.env2.observe`, `html.env2-js` guard, reduced-motion = all lit) must load once on every `hdt-page-type-*`; the index gradient stays index-only.
2. **Give every non-index template a ground** (HANDOFF §3: probably sky-3 `#0f1a2f` / sky-4 `#070b15`). Today `settings_data.json` `color_scheme_body: "scheme-1"` (`#f7f0e6`/`#2b2118`) paints every side page, Kalles paints `#wrapper/main`, and every page ends in a hard cream→`#020306` cut at the footer. Either a per-`body.hdt-page-type-*` rule in the core or a minimal `settings_data.json` edit (never a rewrite).
3. **Settle the header once.** `sections/header-group.json`: `header_transparent: true`, `background_opacity_transparent: 0`, `header_colors`/`header_transparent_colors`/`header_sticky_colors`/`header_menu_colors`/`header_mobile_colors` all `scheme-env2-night`, `sticky_type: "on_scroll_up"`, `background_opacity_sticky: 0.9`. `enabled-transparent-header` is present on every page, so ink `#f4eee3` menu/search/cart/hamburger sit on cream at ≈1.03:1 — invisible on all 30 mirrored side pages; only the gold logo (`ElmsNest_Logo_Night.png`, 50 px) and the glow "0" badge show. Fix = dark first section on every template (the first section's schema `class` must include `section-allow-transparent hdt-section`; `main-heading`/`top-list-collections` do not) or index-scoped transparency.
4. **Remove or replace the Kalles bands** — `main-heading` (scheme `scheme-77e4ef58…`, `bg_overlay: 54`, sr-only h1 at l.50 that duplicates the editorial h1) and `top-list-collections` in `collection.json`, `search.json`, `cart.json`, `list-collections.json`, `page.json`, `page.contact-us.json`, all `customers/*`.
5. **Footer group** (`footer-group.json`, `scheme-env2-night`) is the only on-system element and stays; but its copyright link "תנאי משלוח וביטול" → `/#env2-terms` (a homepage anchor), its collection names ("גרילנדות ודקורטיבי", "ספוטים ופרוז׳קטורים") differ from the menu, and the sky must reach `#020306` before it on every template.
6. **Back-to-top**: `system-group.json` `back_top` (`border_cl: "#d2cbc1"`, `border_cl_active: "#2b2118"`, old palette) renders on every page and is hidden on index by the base snippet — hide everywhere or re-token.
7. **WhatsApp float**: `snippets/elmsnest-whatsapp.liquid` is already rendered at `theme.liquid` l.207 on every template; it outputs nothing because `settings.whatsapp_number` is absent from `settings_data.json` `current`. Every "שלחו תמונה / לשליחת תמונה של המקום" CTA therefore resolves to `/pages/contact-us`, whose Shopify form has no file field.
8. **Type**: `font_family_shopify_1/2/3: "assistant_n4"`, `hd_fweight: 600`, `font_base: "--f_family_1"` — the core must set `--f_family_*`/Heebo for Kalles chrome (header menu, drawer, quick-add popup, facets) or those stay Assistant next to Heebo sections.
